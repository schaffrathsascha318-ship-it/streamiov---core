# Streamiov Rights Acquisition Pipeline

## Mission

Streamiov does not plan to buy entire publishers, studios, or game catalogues as its default route. The preferred route is to obtain specific written permissions or licences from current rights holders or authorised successors so Streamiov can lawfully create and publish new editions of dormant or discontinued games.

A title being inactive, abandoned in practice, delisted, commercially unavailable, or no longer remembered by the market is a discovery signal only. It does not itself create permission, public-domain status, or an orphan-work exception.

## Target permissions

For each title, identify and request only the rights needed for the intended project:
- develop and publish a remake;
- remaster, restore, port, or modernise for new platforms;
- create a genuine 2D-to-3D adaptation;
- develop sequels, expansions, or new episodes;
- use title, logo, characters, story, music, artwork, code, and other components only where expressly included in the grant;
- distribute, market, localise, update, and maintain the new edition;
- define territory, term, exclusivity, sublicensing, revenue terms, credit, approvals, and termination.

Prefer a narrow, affordable, non-exclusive development licence where it meets the project need. Do not assume that a licence to one component includes any other component.

## Discovery scope

Search worldwide, with initial release-year coverage 1996–2026 and platform coverage including N64, PlayStation generations, SEGA Mega Drive/Saturn/Dreamcast, Nintendo handhelds and consoles, Xbox generations, Atari, PC/DOS, arcade, mobile, and browser games.

Prioritise games no longer actively developed or commercially supported; studios that closed, were acquired, dissolved, or entered insolvency; franchises without a recent mainline release; catalogues whose rights chain may have fragmented; original developers or publishers who publicly invited licensing or preservation; and titles with an identifiable current rights holder and plausible contact route.

Do not label a title abandoned, orphaned, ownerless, or free without evidence. Record the exact observed fact and date instead.

## Rights-chain research

For every candidate, investigate separately: original developer and publisher; studio closure, acquisition, insolvency, asset sale, and corporate successor records; current owner of copyright in code and game content; authorship, co-authorship, employment/commission terms, and estate/succession where relevant; trademarks, title/logo rights, registered designs, patents where applicable; music, voice, fonts, middleware, licensed brands, and third-party content; territorial differences and term calculations; public statements and primary documents that establish grants or transfers.

A deceased creator does not make a work free. Identify the estate, heirs, employer, publisher, assignee, or other successor. A dissolved company does not necessarily mean its assets or rights have no owner. Escalate unclear chains for human legal review.

## Status model

- DISCOVERY: lead found; no legal conclusion.
- DORMANT_CANDIDATE: evidence indicates no current active development/support; no permission implied.
- RIGHTS_HOLDER_IDENTIFIED: current likely owner/successor identified with evidence.
- CONTACT_ROUTE_FOUND: official licensing/legal contact or authorised representative found.
- PERMISSION_REQUEST_PREPARED: title-specific scope and request drafted.
- NEGOTIATION: contact or negotiation documented.
- PERMISSION_GRANTED: signed written permission/licence reviewed for scope and conditions.
- PARTIAL_CLEARANCE: only named components/activities cleared.
- PUBLIC_DOMAIN_VERIFIED: term and scope verified for relevant territory and components.
- BLOCKED: rights unclear, conflicting, refused, or insufficient.
- ARCHIVED: files lawfully obtained and stored with provenance and integrity hashes.
- PRODUCTION_READY: all rights needed for the exact planned production and release are documented.

DORMANT_CANDIDATE is a market/research status, never a legal clearance status. PERMISSION_GRANTED must link to a signed agreement and reviewed rights matrix. Never infer permission from silence or non-response.

## Permission request dossier

Prepare a concise dossier for the user's legal review and outreach: title, release year, platforms, original credits; documented inactivity/support status and last known release/update; rights-chain diagram with primary-source citations; known exclusions and third-party rights; requested adaptations and platforms; exact components requested; proposed licence term, territory, exclusivity, sublicensing and commercial terms; attribution, quality-control, approval, preservation and termination proposals; evidence log, contact attempts, responses, and agreement version.

The user handles legal decisions, negotiation, and execution of agreements. The AI may research, organise evidence, draft non-binding request materials, and prepare comparison matrices; it must not claim rights have been obtained or send legal commitments without explicit user instruction.

## Production and archival gates

- Do not download, mirror, redistribute, or build derivative works from protected game files until applicable permission is documented.
- Public repository access is not a licence.
- A source-code licence does not automatically cover game data, art, audio, names, characters, or trademarks.
- If only mechanics or general ideas can be used, prepare a clean-room original game without copying protected expression, branding, code, or assets.
- Store only metadata and source references in the public repository until binary archival rights are confirmed.
- Every release candidate must have a component-by-component rights matrix and agreement/provenance record.

## Deliverables

1. A broad, deduplicated master catalogue of candidates.
2. Evidence-backed dormant/inactive status per title.
3. Rights-holder and successor research per title.
4. Contact route and permission dossier per title.
5. Separate production queue for remake, remaster, sequel, port, and 2D-to-3D candidates.
6. Explicit blocked/cleared status; no invented permissions or rights conclusions.
